# Arms Demo: Study Notes

**Course:** Personal Training Certification

**Topic:** Curl variations, triceps exercises by problem area, wrist curls and grip exercises

**About these notes:** Short revision version of the Arms Demo. For the full explanation, see the Detailed Notes. The muscle theory is in the Arms Lecture notes.

**Big idea:** Choose the exercise that avoids MAI and RI for the target muscle, and match the grip to the muscle. No exercise is "bad", but some train a muscle better.

---

## 1. Elbow flexion

- **Recommended:** standing dumbbell curl with supination (elbow flexion plus supination). Hold the dumbbell off centre.
- **Hammer curl:** brachioradialis. **Reverse curl:** brachialis. **Barbell and EZ bar curl:** biceps (EZ bar is kinder to the wrist).
- **MAI (shoulder flexed):** high pulley, concentration and spider curls.
- **Zottman curl:** switching grip means neither muscle does a full repetition.
- **21s:** 14 half repetitions are junk volume. Full range is needed for hypertrophy.
- **Cable curl:** face away so the resistance is highest where the biceps is strongest.

## 2. Triceps

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| 1 (MAI) | Kickback |
| 2A (compound RI) | Close grip bench, parallel dips, bench dips, pushdown |
| 2B (isolation RI) | Skull crusher, overhead and lying cable extension |

- **Recommended:** standing cable triceps extension, arms by the sides. No RI, no MAI, long head strongest at zero degrees.
- Supinated grip makes it a reverse extension. A rope does not change the focus. Choose the grip the wrist tolerates.
- Dips: upright for triceps, forward for chest.

## 3. Wrist and grip

- **Wrist curl** (supinated): wrist flexion. **Reverse wrist curl** (pronated): wrist extension.
- Hurdles: MAI, carpal tunnel syndrome, and RI for the reverse curl. These matter after the beginner stage.
- **Length changing:** captains of crush, gorilla gripper.
- **Isometric:** dead hang, farmer's walk (trap bar), pinch hold (smooth plates), fat grips (a prop).

---

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Junk volume | Repetitions that add fatigue but no hypertrophy benefit |
| Carpal tunnel syndrome | Compression of the nerve in the wrist tunnel, painful in wrist flexion |
| Reverse curl | Pronated curl (flexion) or supinated extension (triceps) |
| Isometric | Muscle length stays the same while tension changes |

## Revision questions

1. What is the recommended elbow flexion exercise and why?
2. Why hold the dumbbell off centre in the supination curl?
3. Which curls carry MAI?
4. What is wrong with the Zottman curl?
5. What is wrong with 21s?
6. Why face away in a cable curl?
7. Which triceps exercises are category 2A?
8. Why does the cable extension work for the long head?
9. What are the hurdles of the wrist curl?
10. Name two isometric and two length changing grip exercises.

## Answers

1. The standing dumbbell curl with supination: elbow flexion and supination, no MAI.
2. So the turning action also meets resistance.
3. High pulley, concentration and spider curls (shoulder held flexed).
4. The grip switch gives the biceps a concentric and the brachialis an eccentric, so neither gets a full repetition.
5. Half repetitions are junk volume and the muscle is tired for the full ones.
6. Resistance is highest low in the range where the biceps is strongest.
7. Close grip bench, parallel dips, bench dips and cable pushdown.
8. No shoulder flexion, no MAI, and the long head is strongest at zero degrees of elevation.
9. MAI, carpal tunnel syndrome and (reverse curl) RI.
10. Isometric: dead hang, farmer's walk. Length changing: captains of crush, gorilla gripper.
